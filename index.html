🏫 2026 UC Personal Insight Questions (PIQs)

Pick 4 of 8

The UC application requires 4 essays (350 words each) from the 8 prompts below. Choose the ones where you have the most specific, authentic story.

PIQ #1
Describe an example of your leadership experience in which you have positively influenced others, helped resolve disputes, or contributed to group efforts over time.
💡 Tip: Leadership doesn't require a title. Think about moments you took initiative or brought people together.
PIQ #2
Every person has a creative side, and it can be expressed in many ways: problem solving, original and innovative thinking, and artistically, to name a few. Describe how you express your creative side.
💡 Tip: Creativity includes engineering, cooking, fashion, coding, or solving a community problem.
PIQ #3
What would you say is your greatest talent or skill? How have you developed and demonstrated that talent over time?
💡 Tip: Be specific — describe a concrete moment that reveals your talent, not just what it is.
PIQ #4
Describe how you have taken advantage of a significant educational opportunity or worked to overcome an educational barrier you have faced.
💡 Tip: Great place to mention programs from this site — USC YRP, Riordan, etc. — or overcome challenges.
PIQ #5
Describe the most significant challenge you have faced and the steps you have taken to overcome this challenge. How has this challenge affected your academic achievement?
💡 Tip: Authenticity matters more than a "perfect" story. Show how you grew from the challenge.
PIQ #6
Think about an academic subject that inspires you. Describe how you have furthered this interest inside and/or outside of the classroom.
💡 Tip: Connect your academic interest to real action you've taken — research, a project, community work.
PIQ #7
What have you done to make your school or your community a better place?
💡 Tip: Volunteer work from this site's programs (UCLA Health, LAPL, Cedars-Sinai) is perfect here. Use numbers!
PIQ #8
Beyond what has already been shared in your application, what do you want us to know about you?
💡 Tip: Use this to fill a gap — bilingüismo, family responsibilities, or something unique not mentioned elsewhere.
UC PIQ Strategy: Choose 4 that each highlight a DIFFERENT side of you. Don't repeat themes. Avoid #8 unless truly necessary. Short sentences — 350 words goes fast. Write in your own natural voice.

📝 Common App Personal Statement (2026)

650 Words Max

The Common App personal statement (650 words max) goes to ALL Common App schools — USC, LMU, most privates. Choose your strongest prompt.

Prompt 1 Background & Identity
Some students have a background, identity, interest, or talent that is so meaningful they believe their application would be incomplete without it. If this sounds like you, then please share your story.
💡 Great for first-gen, immigrant, or bilingual students. Navigating two cultures is a compelling story.
Prompt 2 Overcoming Obstacles
The lessons we take from obstacles we encounter can be fundamental to later success. Recount a time when you faced a challenge, setback, or failure. How did it affect you, and what did you learn?
💡 Focus on growth and transformation, not just how hard things were. Show what you did with the experience.
Prompt 3 Challenging a Belief
Reflect on a time when you questioned or challenged a belief or idea. What prompted your thinking? What was the outcome?
💡 Shows intellectual maturity. Great if you changed your mind about something through a real experience.
Prompt 4 Problem Solving
Reflect on something that someone has done for you that has made you happy or thankful in a surprising way. How has this gratitude affected or motivated you?
💡 Underused — a genuine mentor or family story here can be incredibly moving and memorable.
Prompt 5 Personal Growth
Discuss an accomplishment, event, or realization that sparked a period of personal growth and a new understanding of yourself or others.
💡 Very popular prompt. Make the growth feel earned through a specific story, not abstract claims.
Prompt 6 What Captivates You
Describe a topic, idea, or concept you find so engaging that it makes you lose all track of time. Why does it captivate you? What or who do you turn to when you want to learn more?
💡 Avoid clichés — start with the specific moment you fell in love with the topic, not a generic origin story.
Prompt 7 ⭐ Your Own Topic (Most Freedom!)
Share an essay on any topic of your choice. It can be one you've already written, one that responds to a different prompt, or one of your own design.
💡 Best for students with a truly unique story. If none of Prompts 1–6 feel natural — use this one.
Common App Essay Strategy: Start with a specific scene — not a quote or generic statement. The officer should picture where you are in sentence one. Reveal something NOT shown elsewhere. Read it aloud — if it doesn't sound like you, rewrite it.
