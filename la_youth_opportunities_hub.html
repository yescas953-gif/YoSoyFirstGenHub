✅ Resume Tips for Students

01
Keep it to 1 page — always.
02
Use action verbs: Led, Created, Managed.
03
"200+ students reached" beats "helped students."
04
Tailor each resume to the program's keywords.
05
Include GPA if 3.0+ and strong AP/IB courses.
06
Have a counselor or teacher review before submitting.
