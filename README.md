# LA Youth Opportunities 🌟

## 📌 Overview

LA Youth Opportunities is a website designed to help high school students in Los Angeles easily find internships, volunteer opportunities, free programs, and career resources.

The goal is to make opportunities more accessible—especially for students who may not know where to start.

---

## 🎯 Features

* 📅 **Upcoming Deadlines Tracker** – Stay on top of important application dates
* 💼 **Paid Internships** – Curated opportunities for students
* 🎓 **Free Programs** – Enrichment and academic programs
* ❤️ **Volunteer Opportunities** – Ways to give back and gain experience
* 📝 **Resume & Application Help** – Resources for essays, resumes, and interviews
* 🔗 **Helpful Resources Section** – College, career, and job tools
